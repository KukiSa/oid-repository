# Privacy Policy
*Version 1.2.0. Release date: November 6, 2026.*

Welcome to the Signaliks OID Repository. We highly value your privacy and data security. Please read the following privacy terms carefully before continuing to browse this website:

## 1. Use of Cookies (Content Provider)
As the content provider of this website, we commit that during your visit, we will not actively write, store, or read any cookies on your device, nor will we use similar technologies to track your personal behavior.

## 2. Website Delivery
Request data may be processed as necessary to deliver pages, maintain availability, and protect the service. This data is not used to build behavioral advertising profiles.

## 3. Collection and Retention of Access Logs
For website operation, security, abuse prevention, and performance diagnosis, access logs may include IP address, browser User Agent, requested path, and request time. Access to these logs is restricted to operational use. Retention and access are governed by operational needs and applicable obligations.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal data held by Signaliks. Requests may be sent to `contact@iks.moe`. Some provider-controlled security logs may not be directly searchable or deletable by Signaliks; the response will identify what data is controlled by Signaliks and what action can be taken.

## 5. Updates to the Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to adapt to changes in laws and regulations or updates to our service practices. All revisions will be published directly on this page. By continuing to access or use this website after updates to this Policy have been made, you acknowledge and accept the revised Privacy Policy.
