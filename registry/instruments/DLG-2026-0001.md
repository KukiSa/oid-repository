# Delegation Instrument DLG-2026-0001

## Parties

- Delegator: Signaliks OID Registration Authority (`signaliks-root-ra`)
- Delegate: Youngdo PKI Registration Authority (`youngdo-pki-ra`)

## Delegated Naming Domain

`1.3.6.1.4.1.65553.2`

## Scope

Within the delegated naming domain, the Delegate may allocate immediate nodes and sub-arcs, adopt subordinate policies, and publish registration records. All actions remain subject to the Signaliks root registry policy and must be represented by approved registration decisions in the structured registry.

Subdelegation is not authorized by this instrument. A separate instrument approved by the Delegator is required before any third party may act as a subordinate Registration Authority.

## Continuity and Termination

The delegation became effective on April 16, 2026 and was recorded and ratified in the structured registry on September 10, 2026. Suspension, revocation, expiry, or transfer must be recorded as a new event and must not erase or invalidate lawful historical allocations.

On termination, registry custody and unpublished pending requests return to the Delegator unless a replacement instrument states otherwise. The Delegate must provide the records required to preserve uniqueness, provenance, and operational continuity.

## Governing Records

The machine-readable delegation record in `registry/registry.json` is authoritative for status, dates, permissions, and policy references. This document explains the administrative effect of that record.
